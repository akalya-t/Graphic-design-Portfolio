About Me

Design is how
I think

I'm Akalya Thirumurugan, a brand designer and visual communicator based in Chennai, India. I started on Upwork creating logos for businesses across fitness, food, faith, and lifestyle — learning early that the best design communicates meaning before it decorates anything.

Over the years I've grown into social media design, print materials, stationery, and presentations — always led by the same instinct: clarity over complexity, intention over decoration.

I hold a B.Sc B.Ed in Mathematics from Central University of Tamil Nadu, and I'm currently self-funding my Masters through freelance design. I'm actively available for brand and graphic design projects.
